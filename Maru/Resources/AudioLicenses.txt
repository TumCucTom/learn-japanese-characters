Native Kana Audio Notices
=========================

This app bundles native-speaker kana pronunciation audio for offline playback.

Wikimedia Commons Public Domain Audio
-------------------------------------

Most basic, dakuten, and handakuten kana audio files are public-domain MP3
transcodes from Wikimedia Commons. The bundled files are derived from public
domain OGG/OGA recordings by Hakatanoshio117117 and Doughaque.

Representative sources:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Audio_files_of_hiragana_(set_by_Hakatanoshio117117)
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ja-ka.ogg

Per-file source URLs and hashes are recorded in AudioSourceManifest.json.

Just Gojuon Audio
-----------------

Combination kana audio files that were not available from the public-domain
Commons set are derived from Just Gojuon:

https://github.com/veardk/just-gojuon

Fallback Audio
--------------

The rare kana sounds "d" and "vu" intentionally use the system Japanese
speech voice when no native bundled recording is available.
