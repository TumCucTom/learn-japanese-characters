Native Kana Audio Notices
=========================

This app bundles native-speaker kana pronunciation audio for offline playback.

Just Gojuon Audio
-----------------

Supported native kana audio files are derived from Just Gojuon:

https://github.com/veardk/just-gojuon

The bundled files use the upstream MP3 dataset published under:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/veardk/just-gojuon/main/public/sounds/index.json

Per-file source URLs, byte counts, and hashes are recorded in
AudioSourceManifest.json.

Fallback Audio
--------------

The rare kana sounds "d" (dakuten iteration mark) and "vu" intentionally use
the system Japanese speech voice when no native bundled recording is available.
